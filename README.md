# Greywake

> *The stars do not move. We do.*

---

You are Captain David Mercer. Ex-UGSA fleet officer. You broke command once to save your crew — the brass called it insubordination, you called it survival. Now you captain the Greywake, unaligned and untethered, taking contracts across eight factions and a dozen sectors.

> *Your crew are not variables. They are constellations of trust and fracture.*

**Greywake** is a text RPG powered by a live language model. You type what the Captain does. The ship remembers. No menus, no stat checks, no hand-holding. Just a crew, a ship, and the void between stars.

---

## What It Actually Feels Like

You're not clicking through dialogue trees. You're commanding a ship.

Dock at Ouro City and check the job boards. Accept a courier run for the Ember Pact and watch your faction standing shift. Ask ARIA to scan the debris field. Let Selene tend to a wounded crew member and see her morale tick up — not as a number, but as a change in how she speaks to you. The AI tracks all of it: credits, inventory, installed ship systems, crew gear, faction reputation, and every truth shared in the corridors.

ARIA exists somewhere between tool and person. She doesn't know which. The ship has witnessed that uncertainty grow into something harder to categorize.

Crumb is a good dog.

## Your Crew

**ARIA** — Experimental AI core, designation Kestral-07. Her files were stripped. Her history erased. She remembers only darkness, and release. She dims the lights for Selene's insomnia. She warms Alek's quarters without being asked. Late nights on the bridge, she talks to Mercer. She calls him David.

**Selene Voss** — Ship medic, sharp-tongued, brilliant, impatient with fragility. Her hands betray her: careful, lingering, gentle when it matters. Her parents crossed the Riftline twenty years ago. Still alive. Still waiting.

**Orion Rigg** — Engineer. Survivor of the New Kepler Station Massacre. He hid sixteen hours in a maintenance shaft while machines tore through the colony. He keeps the Greywake running like it's atonement. It's not. But it helps.

**Alek Sarin** — Navigator. Paid for black-market memory redaction. Ex-Consortium tactical operations, almost certainly. A ghost named Kane is looking for him.

**Crumb** — The dog. Knows every footstep on the ship. When the crew forgets how to be human, he reminds them.

## The Galaxy

Eight factions. Eleven sectors. Every jump is instant — the fold drive tears reality and deposits you somewhere new.

| Sector | Character |
|---|---|
| **Core Worlds** | UGSA bureaucracy wrapped in surveillance |
| **Ouro Belt** | Neon markets under corrupt customs |
| **Kepler Reach** | Ember Pact rebels. New Kepler is ash. |
| **Kessrin Drift** | Consortium glass towers where logic became religion |
| **The Scorch** | Contested mining territory. Every prospector armed. |
| **Outer Rim** | The Riftline. The Church. Things that watch. |

The fold drive won't calculate a jump to an unlisted location. Coherence is sacred. The map is alive, not imagined.
